Replace WooCommerce checkout with Stripe Checkout quickly. Let your customers pay with confidence using highly optimized, Stripe hosted checkout.

== Description ==
This plugin is the most extensive integration between WooCommerce and Stripe Checkout, developed to improve your online store's revenues by giving more confidence to the customers to checkout on your store. It effortlessly connects your WooCommerce store with Stripe Checkout bypassing the WooCommerce checkout completely. You can customize the Stripe Checkout displayed to your customers by using the settings available in the plugin. All possible options are available in the premium version along with some additional features to review the Stripe checkout sessions created on your website.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==
= Are fees, shipping and taxes included in Stripe Checkout? =
Yes. Stripe Checkout uses the saved WooCommerce order total, including eligible discounts, fees, shipping and taxes. Positive fees, shipping charges and taxes are shown separately. WooCommerce calculates these amounts before Stripe Checkout opens; changing an address on Stripe Checkout does not recalculate WooCommerce shipping or taxes.

= Can I use WooCommerce coupons in the free version? =
Yes. To use coupons, enable "Enable the use of coupon codes" under WooCommerce > Settings > General and save your changes. The plugin respects this setting; it does not enable or disable coupons automatically. Customers apply coupon codes in the WooCommerce cart before opening Stripe Checkout, and eligible discounts are included in the payment total. This does not add coupon-code entry to Stripe Checkout. Coupon eligibility and usage restrictions still apply.

Coupons limited to one use per customer require login. For higher per-customer limits, guests must have a valid billing email available before applying the coupon; otherwise, they must log in.

= How does it bypass WooCommerce default checkout? =
 Once everything is ready and the plugin is enabled, any customer who clicks a Checkout link or button from anywhere of your website, is redirected to Stripe Checkout. Therefore, you don't need to worry about how your WooCommerce checkout works or looks because no customer will land on that after enabling this plugin.

= I have installed the plugin but my plugin appears disabled =
Get your Test/Live Stripe keys and webhook keys from the Stripe account, enter those keys in the Stripe Settings tab and Click on the Enable button.

=  I want to add custom payment methods to my Stripe Checkout =

== External services ==

This plugin uses Stripe Checkout to process payments and requires a Stripe account. After you configure Stripe API credentials, the plugin contacts Stripe to verify the account connection and create, retrieve or expire checkout sessions and verify their payment status.

Requests include order and item references, verification metadata, product names and public image URLs when available, quantities, totals, discounts, fees, shipping, taxes, currency, store return URLs and the billing email when available. Customers enter payment details directly on Stripe's hosted page. Stripe may also collect their name, billing address, shipping address and phone number according to the checkout settings. The plugin receives payment status and customer details from Stripe to update the WooCommerce order.

See the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) and [Stripe Services Agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa).
